Method

Four gates. Nothing advances without the numbers behind it.

GATE 01

Survey & Specification

Site survey, requirements capture and a written specification both sides sign before any design work begins.

GATE 02

Design & Calculation

Schematics, simulations and sizing calculations issued as numbered drawings under revision control.

GATE 03

Build & Integration

Installation or prototyping by our own crews, with factory and site acceptance tests against the spec.

Measured results, as-built drawings and the full compliance file — then a signature, not a shrug.
